Assumption-by-Assumption Scope & Justification

The ME361 project locks the scope strictly to pure orthogonal cutting, single shear plane, steady state. The table below details every slide assumption from Lecture 2, whether real data violate it, the physical cause, and the twin's rigorous handling.

Lecture-2 Slide Assumption Physical Reality (ETH Data) Digital Twin Handling Scope Status
1. Perfectly sharp tool (cutting edge radius rn = 0, no flank contact) Real inserts have finite hone (ETH rn ≈ 38 µm). At small t, forces show large positive intercepts at t → 0 due to ploughing. Calibrated edge force terms Ke_z (≈13–15 N/mm) added to cutting force. CORRECTED
2. Constant mean friction coefficient μ on the tool rake face Apparent μ = F/N falls from ~1.8 at t=0.01 mm down to ~0.5 at t=0.2 mm as ploughing gives way to chip flow. Fitted thickness sensitivity n_mu (μ ∝ t-n_mu). Corrects the scale effect without breaking steady state. CORRECTED
3. Material shear strength τs = σy/√3 (initial yield) Shear plane undergoes severe plastic deformation (shear strain γ ≈ 2–3). Work-hardening steels (Ck45, 316L) harden significantly. Calibration multiplier tau_k (Ck45: 2.66, SS316L provisional: 5.44) matches Johnson-Cook flow stress at true shear strain. CORRECTED
4. Continuous chip formation (no segmentation, no serration) Ti6Al4V forms segmented/serrated chips at all tested speeds. Ck45 forms continuous chips above 150 m/min. Ck45 serves as the continuous-chip steel analogue for SS316L. Segmented Ti6Al4V is documented as an illustrative violation. IN-SCOPE (CONTINUOUS)
5. No Built-Up Edge (BUE) Ck45 chips show severe BUE below vc = 100 m/min and at vc = 150 m/min for small feeds. Every BUE-suspect row is explicitly dropped by datapipe.apply_scope and logged. Only stable, clean cuts retained. FILTERED OUT
6. Isothermal cutting (no thermal softening / temperature fields) At high cutting speeds, heat softens the workpiece, slightly reducing cutting forces. Speed terms (c_rate, m_mu) are left OFF in the default preset because temperature models are strictly out of scope. OUT OF SCOPE
7. Steady state within a run (no start-up/exit transients) Forces reach a steady plateau after ~1–2 ms of engagement. Forces remain mathematically constant across time; live feed demonstrates steady-state feed per slide equations. LOCKED SCOPE
